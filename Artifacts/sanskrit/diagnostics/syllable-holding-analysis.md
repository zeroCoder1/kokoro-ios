# Syllable, weight and holding diagnostics

Derived algorithmically from `SanskritSyllabifier`. Rules from
sanskritguide.com/chapter-4-syllables — one vowel per syllable; light iff it
ends in a short vowel; heavy on a long vowel, a closing consonant, an anusvāra
or a visarga. Heavy is two mātrās, light one.

`HOLD` lists the syllables whose closing consonant opens a written conjunct —
the "half letter" the reference says to give ample time. Holding is duration
and clear articulation, never an inserted vowel.
